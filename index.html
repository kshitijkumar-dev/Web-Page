FUNDAMENTALS

Core Concepts

Before a drug reaches a patient, thousands of molecules are tested virtually. Here's what makes that possible.

🔬

What is In Silico Drug Design?

Using computers to predict, design, and optimize drug candidates before any lab work begins — saving years and billions of dollars in early-stage research.

🧲

Molecular Docking

A computational method that predicts the preferred orientation of a drug molecule (ligand) when it binds to a protein target. Lower binding energy = stronger interaction.

⚡

Binding Free Energy

The thermodynamic measure (kcal/mol) of how strongly a drug binds. Calculated from electrostatic, van der Waals, and hydrogen-bond interactions at the active site.

🧪

ADMET Properties

Absorption · Distribution · Metabolism · Excretion · Toxicity — five critical filters every drug candidate must pass before clinical trials.

🤖

AI & Machine Learning

Deep learning models (GNNs, Transformers) trained on millions of known drug–target interactions can predict binding affinity and ADMET profiles in milliseconds.

💊

Lipinski's Rule of Five

A drug-likeness filter: MW ≤ 500 Da, logP ≤ 5, H-bond donors ≤ 5, H-bond acceptors ≤ 10. Molecules that violate ≥ 2 rules often have poor oral bioavailability.
